Australia declared war on birds.
Nineteen thirty-two. Western Australia. Wheat farmers begged the government — emus were eating the harvest.
So the Defence Minister sent two soldiers, a Lewis gun, and ten thousand rounds.
The emus didn't run. They scattered. Every burst hit dust.
The major tried ambushes. He tried mounting the gun on a truck — the vibration shook the sights loose.
A farmer joked the emus were reading the field manual.
But when the ammunition ran out, the birds were still there.
The army withdrew. The emus stayed for dinner.
